Neosis Privacy Notice

Effective July 21, 2026

Neosis messages and files are encrypted in transit, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. The application operator and database provider can access stored content.

Data Neosis processes

Neosis stores your Google-verified email, display name, profile status, contacts, messages, uploaded media, conversation preferences, privacy settings, reports, blocked-user records, and server-side login sessions. Login history contains a general device/browser label and a masked network address, not a full IP address.

How data is used

Data is used to authenticate you, deliver chat features, apply your privacy choices, prevent abuse, investigate reports, and keep the service reliable. Neosis does not use your Google password, passkeys, or two-factor secrets.

Sharing and processors

Render hosts the application and MongoDB stores application data. Google provides OAuth authentication. Data may be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect users and the service.

Retention and controls

  • Login history expires after 180 days.
  • Disappearing text messages are removed after their configured expiry, subject to database cleanup timing. Attachments are not currently covered by disappearing-message timers.
  • You can export your data, clear chats for your account, revoke sessions, unblock users, or permanently delete your account from Settings.
  • Account deletion removes reports you submitted. Reports about your account may be retained under a de-identified account marker when needed for abuse prevention.
  • Recipients may retain copies, exports, screenshots, or downloaded files even after you clear or delete your own copy.

Security and reports

Do not send vulnerability details through public issues. Follow the repository security policy for private reporting. Abuse reports retain relevant user and message identifiers so they can be investigated.

Contact

Contact the Neosis repository owner through GitHub for privacy requests or questions.
